About

Built to be read on the trailhead

Most hiking pages break down on a phone, exactly where people read them. This one is built the other way around.

01

Structure first

Semantic sections, one clear heading level per block and spacing that holds up from 320 pixels to a wide monitor.

02

Reusable system

Colors, spacing and card styles live in custom properties, so a change in one place moves the whole page.

03

Theme toggle

The button in the header flips between light and dark, and the choice is remembered the next time you open the page.

Trail advice

Quick hiking tips

Start early, wear the right shoes, check conditions and pack more water than you think you need. The mountain does not care about your schedule.

  • Check the weather

    Mountain weather turns fast. Layer up and look at the forecast the morning of, not the night before.

  • Bring the essentials

    Water, snacks and a charged phone belong in the pack every time, even on a one hour trail.

  • Know your route

    Read the distance, the elevation gain and recent trail conditions before you leave the house.
